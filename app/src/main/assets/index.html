FileBandit

Connecting…
Lost connection to the Android phone. Make sure both phones are still on the same Wi-Fi and the FileBandit app is open on the Android phone. This page reconnects by itself.
1

Send to the Android phone

Pick what to send. Files are saved on the Android phone in Downloads › FileBandit. Keep this page open until the bars turn green. If the connection drops, sending carries on where it stopped. On a computer you can also drag files onto this page.

Tip: Files app sends photos and videos exactly as they are. Photos & videos may let iOS convert HEIC photos to JPEG.

2

Get files from the Android phone

Files the Android phone is sharing show up here. Tap Download, then Download again in the Safari prompt. Find them in the Files app under Downloads.

Tips for fast, reliable transfers
  • Keep both screens on and stay on this page while files are moving.
  • 5 GHz Wi-Fi is much faster than 2.4 GHz. On the iPhone: Settings › Wi-Fi › tap your network and check that “Low Data Mode” is off.
  • To save a photo or video into the iPhone Photos app, tap Save to Photos next to it.
  • If this page stops working, scan the QR code on the Android phone again with the Camera app.
